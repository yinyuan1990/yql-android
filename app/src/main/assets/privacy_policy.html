I. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

(1) Principles of Collection

We follow the principles of legitimacy, lawfulness and necessity when collecting and obtaining your personal information. When you use our services, the personal information we may need to collect and use falls into the following two categories:

1. To provide you with the basic functions of our products or services, you must authorize us to collect and use the necessary information. If you refuse to provide such information, you will not be able to use our products or services normally;

2. To provide you with additional functions of our services, you may choose to authorize us to collect and use the corresponding information.

(2) Purposes of Use

1. To help you complete registration

When you register, you need to provide us with at least your own mobile phone number. We will verify your identity by sending an SMS verification code.

2. To provide you with products or services

To deliver products or services to you, you need to provide the recipient's identity information, name, delivery address, contact phone number and other information.

3. To send you messages

We may use your information to send you marketing messages and to provide or promote our products and services.

4. To provide security protection

To provide you with better security, we may use your information for identity verification, customer service, security prevention, fraud monitoring and similar purposes.

II. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

To keep the software running properly, give you an easier browsing experience and recommend content that may interest you, we store small data files called cookies on your device or system. A cookie usually contains an identifier, a site name and some numbers and characters.

You can manage or delete cookies according to your preferences. You can clear all cookies saved on your computer, and most browsers provide a function to block cookies.

III. How We Share, Transfer or Publicly Disclose Your Personal Information

Except in the following cases, we will not share your personal information with any third party outside Golden Phoenix without your consent:

  • Sharing required by law
  • Providing our services to you
  • Maintaining and improving our services
  • Sharing based on your explicit consent

IV. How We Protect Your Personal Information

We adopt security measures that meet industry standards, including reasonable rules and policies and security technologies, to prevent unauthorized access, use or modification of your information and to avoid data damage or loss.

Our network services use a variety of encryption technologies. For example, in some services we use encryption (such as SSL) to protect your information, store your information in encrypted form, and isolate it using isolation technologies.

V. How You Can Manage Your Personal Information

You have the right to access your personal information, except as otherwise provided by laws and regulations. You can access it yourself in the following ways:

  • Account information - if you want to view or edit the profile information in your account
  • Search information - you can view or clear your search history
  • Order information - you can view and manage your orders and records

VI. How We Handle Children's Personal Information

We attach great importance to protecting the personal information of minors. If you are a minor under the age of 18, you should obtain the consent of your parents or other guardians before using our products or services.

We do not actively collect personal information directly from minors. Where personal information of minors is collected with the consent of a guardian, we will only use, share, transfer or disclose it when permitted by law, consented to by the guardian, or necessary to protect the minor.

VII. How This Policy Is Updated

We may revise the terms of this Policy from time to time, and such revisions form part of this Policy. For material changes, we will provide a prominent notice, and you may choose to stop using Golden Phoenix services. If you continue to use Golden Phoenix services in that case, you agree to be bound by the revised Policy.

VIII. How to Contact Us

If you have any complaints or reports about network information security, or any questions, comments or suggestions about this Policy or matters relating to your personal information, we will reply to you as soon as possible after receiving them and verifying your identity.
